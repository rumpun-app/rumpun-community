# Code Provenance

Every contribution must have reviewable provenance.

Contributors confirm that they authored the work or have the right to submit it under AGPL-3.0-only, identify copied or adapted third-party material with source and license, and disclose generated material and its inputs where required by the contribution process.

Do not reproduce, paraphrase, or derive work from confidential, inaccessible, or non-public material. Work only from this repository, cited public standards, and public upstream dependency documentation needed for the task. When provenance is uncertain, stop and request maintainer review.
