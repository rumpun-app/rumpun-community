# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation welcoming and harassment-free regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent without ignoring harm, critique ideas rather than people, accept correction, protect personal data, and make room for contributors with different backgrounds and abilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, discriminatory language, doxxing, publishing private family information, sustained disruption, or retaliation are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the organization's published contact channel. Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants based on severity and pattern. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is inspired by Contributor Covenant 2.1 and may be replaced by the canonical text through community review.
